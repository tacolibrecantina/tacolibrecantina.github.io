About Taco Libre

Chrome on the outside. Neon on the inside. Flavor from everywhere.

The Origin

Elevated tacos, global perspective

Taco Libre was built on a simple idea: the taco is the perfect passport.

Our kitchen starts with ancestral Mexican technique — slow-braised barbacoa, spit-style pastor, carnitas, fresh salsas — then lets the world in. That's how you end up with butter chicken and raita on a tortilla, Nashville hot tenders, Creole shrimp, and a smash burger taco on the same menu.

Every taco is sold individually, so you can build your own flavor journey. Order at the counter, grab a seat under the neon, and we'll bring it to you.
